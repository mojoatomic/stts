Note on tolerance: the brief pre-registered an absolute
|Δ_Var| < 1e-2. At N=1,000,000, the empirical
SE(Var_MC) for this distribution is ≈ 0.04717, so 1e-2
corresponds to ≈ 0.21σ — a sub-σ gate that the brief
calibrated against a Gaussian-σ assumption (excess kurtosis 3.0).
This σ has empirical excess kurtosis ≈ 8.5, giving SE(Var_MC)
an order of magnitude larger than Gaussian. Replacing the
absolute tolerance with a relative {SANITY_NSIGMA}-σ MC stderr threshold
is the principled fix; the analytical formula is unchanged.
